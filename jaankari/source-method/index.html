Sources and Methodology

Where our market data comes from, how records are checked and how each displayed price should be read.

Methodology version: 10 October 2026

Primary price sources

FasalBhav prepares mandi price information by combining published Agmarknet records with independently obtained and verified market records. A record is accepted only when the mandi, crop, minimum price, modal price, maximum price and record date can be matched.

A published Google Sheet may be used as a supplementary source. Its record can replace an Agmarknet record only when every required field matches and its date is the same or newer.

Validation process

Mandi and crop names are matched against maintained lists and approved aliases. A nearby mandi is not treated as the requested mandi. Minimum, modal and maximum prices are kept from the same dated record; values from different dates or sources are not combined.

Records that fail validation are not published as current prices. Previously verified records can remain stored for historical use.

Source priority and conflicts

A complete verified record is preferred over an incomplete row. When two accepted sources contain the same mandi and crop, the newer valid record is used. A supplementary record does not replace a newer published record. Conflicting values are not averaged, and an unresolved conflict is not presented as a confirmed current price.

Minimum, modal and maximum prices

The minimum price is the lower reported value and the maximum price is the upper reported value for the record. The modal price is the representative or most frequently occurring price reported for the market record. It is not necessarily a mathematical average and is not a guaranteed selling price.

Actual bids may differ because of variety, grade, moisture, cleanliness, lot size, arrivals, demand and local trading conditions.

Dates, varieties and units

Every price must be read with its stated date and unit. Crop and state pages use records for the website's current price date. A mandi page uses that date when available; otherwise it may show that mandi's latest verified earlier date.

Named varieties and grades are preserved when supplied. An empty, “Other” or “Others” variety is hidden only when it duplicates an identical named-variety record. Vegetables and fruits may be converted to per-kilogram display, while dry and field crops are generally shown per quintal.

MSP and bullion data

Minimum Support Price information is taken only from government sources and is kept separate from mandi auction prices. Gold and silver reference rates are also separate from agricultural prices and use the stated IBJA reference date and unit. They are not final jewellery prices.

Corrections and limitations

Public source systems may be delayed, revised or temporarily unavailable. FasalBhav does not invent a price to fill a missing record. To report an incorrect mandi, crop, unit, date or price, send the exact page URL and supporting details through Feedback.
