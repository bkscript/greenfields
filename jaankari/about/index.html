About FasalBhav

FasalBhav helps farmers, traders and readers compare agricultural market prices by crop, mandi and state.

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Our purpose

FasalBhav is an independent agricultural information website. We organise mandi price records in a form that is easier to read on mobile and desktop devices. Visitors can find crop-wise, mandi-wise and state-wise information without searching through large raw datasets.

The website is intended for farmers, traders, researchers and other readers who need a practical reference for agricultural market prices in India.

What we publish

Price tables may show the minimum, modal and maximum price reported for a crop in a mandi on the stated date. We also publish explanatory articles about crop quality, cultivation and the correct interpretation of market prices.

Vegetable and fruit prices may be presented per kilogram for easier reading. Field crops are generally presented per quintal. Visitors should always check the unit and date shown with a price.

Editorial principles

We aim to use identifiable sources, preserve the date attached to every price record and avoid presenting estimates as verified prices. A price is not invented to make a table look complete. Different varieties and grades may be shown separately when the source reports them separately.

Corrections and useful additions are reviewed before publication. Our aim is clarity and practical value rather than publishing pages only to capture search traffic.

How coverage is selected

Coverage is expanded when a mandi can be identified correctly and usable records can be verified. We do not substitute a nearby market merely to fill a location page. Crop coverage follows the names, varieties and units found in verified records, with aliases used only to resolve genuine naming differences.

Independence

FasalBhav is not a government department, APMC, commodity exchange, broker or trading platform. References to public agencies, markets and data providers do not imply endorsement or official affiliation.
