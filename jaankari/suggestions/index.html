Feedback and Corrections

Help us correct inaccurate information and improve the usefulness of FasalBhav.

Feedback process reviewed: 10 October 2026

What you can report

You may report an incorrect mandi name, crop name, state, variety, unit, date, price, broken link, missing image or display problem. You may also suggest a mandi, crop or agricultural subject that would be useful to readers.

Details needed for a price correction

Please include the exact page URL, mandi name, crop and variety, record date, minimum price, modal price, maximum price and unit. If possible, include a published source, official notice or clear image that allows the record to be checked.

A message stating only that a price is wrong may not contain enough information for verification.

How submissions are reviewed

Useful submissions are checked against available source information and the identity of the relevant market. A submitted price is not published merely because it was received. Forwarded messages, estimates and unverified quotations are not treated as verified market records.

Submitting feedback does not guarantee publication or an individual reply. Necessary corrections may be made after verification.

What happens after verification

A confirmed factual error may be corrected in the relevant source record or page. A suggestion that cannot yet be verified may be retained for later review, but it will not be presented as confirmed information. Editorial disagreements, promotional requests and requests to manipulate a market price are not accepted as corrections.

Send feedback

You can contact the FasalBhav community through our WhatsApp group. Do not send Aadhaar numbers, bank details, passwords or other sensitive personal information.
