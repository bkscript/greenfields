Privacy Policy

What information may be processed when you visit FasalBhav and how third-party services may use data.

Effective date: 10 October 2026

Information you provide

FasalBhav does not currently provide user accounts or payment facilities. If you voluntarily send feedback through an external service such as WhatsApp, that service may process your name, phone number, profile information and message under its own privacy policy.

Do not submit Aadhaar numbers, bank details, passwords or other sensitive personal information.

Technical and usage information

Standard technical information may be processed automatically when you visit. This can include an IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, pages visited, approximate location, timestamps and diagnostic information. Server and security systems may also create technical logs.

Google Analytics

FasalBhav uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate website usage, including pages visited, traffic sources and device performance. Google may use cookies or similar technologies to provide this service under its own terms and privacy policies.

Google AdSense and advertising

FasalBhav uses Google AdSense technology and may display advertisements when eligible for ad serving. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies to deliver, limit, measure and protect advertising.

Where required, visitors may be offered consent choices through Google's privacy and messaging tools. Advertising preferences may also be managed through Google's advertising settings, subject to regional availability.

Cookies

Cookies are small files or identifiers used for analytics, security, remembering choices and advertising measurement. Browser settings can block or delete cookies, although some website or third-party functions may then work differently.

Your choices

You can restrict cookies through browser settings, use available Google advertising controls and choose not to send feedback through third-party messaging services. Requests concerning information voluntarily sent to FasalBhav should identify the message and the service used so that the request can be assessed.

External links

The website links to services and sources we do not control, including government websites and WhatsApp. Their own privacy policies apply after you open those services.

Retention and security

Technical records may be retained for operational, security, analytics or legal purposes for as long as reasonably necessary. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable steps are taken to avoid unnecessary collection and exposure of private information.

Children's privacy

FasalBhav is a general agricultural information service and is not directed at collecting personal information from children. A parent or guardian can report a concern through the Feedback page.

Policy updates

This policy may be revised when the website, legal requirements or third-party services change. The version published here is the current policy.
